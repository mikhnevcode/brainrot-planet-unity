Platform constraints verified on 2026-10-14

in continuously for 14 days before applying for production access. If that test
starts 2026-10-14, the first eligible application date is no earlier than
2026-10-28; access/review still must be approved. 2026-10-21 can be an APK/internal
test milestone, not a guaranteed public Play launch for this account.

located in Russia. Free apps/updates remain possible. Robokassa alone does not

Mandatory gates BEFORE accepting money/public paid launch
- Real provider adapter and server-side receipt/callback verification.
- Stable player identity, purchase restoration, duplicate/replayed notifications,
  refunds and account deletion/support. Test bearer codes are not production login.
- Tie match identity/loadout to server-verified ownership; replicate effects,
  emotes/profile cosmetics and validate on both phones, not just local decoration.
- Stable HTTPS/WSS hostname, process supervision, backups and health monitoring;
  temporary cloudflare quick tunnels are test infrastructure only.
- Privacy policy, support/deletion URL, Google Data Safety/Apple privacy fields,
  target audience and content rating. Decide mixed-age handling explicitly.
- Child-appropriate purchases/ads and consent design before enabling ad SDKs.
  Ads remain OFF until this is reviewed and device-tested.
- Rights audit of characters, art, fonts, music and third-party logos; original
  commissioned/procedural assets do not automatically clear character/brand rights.
- English localization/store metadata, clear prices and purchase restore UI.
- Device matrix: both Android phones, iPhone, loss/latency, disconnect/reconnect,
  pause/background, cancelled/pending purchases, offline startup, thermal long soak.
- Release signing, backups of keys, AAB/iOS production build, no QA invite/sandbox
  controls in release, and actual store preflight/review.

Do not start real payment transactions, register accounts, accept agreements,
