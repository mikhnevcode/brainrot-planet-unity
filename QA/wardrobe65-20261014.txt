Known boundaries / release work
These are free art-alpha outfits, not a secure storefront. Server-authoritative
ownership, receipts, refunds and entitlement restoration remain future work.
The manifest currently supports at most 128 outfits and a fixed 13-hero rig list.
New heroes/bones/shaders/mechanics require an app update; supported schema-2
outfits can be published with immutable packets and a new manifest without one.
Downloaded disk cache has no automatic eviction yet. Add a production disk budget.
No iOS build, second-phone verification, low-end device matrix or long soak claim.
Do not treat automated forced-victory fixtures as game-balance or store-release QA.
Raw logs/screens remain under ignored Logs/; keys remain outside Git and APK.
